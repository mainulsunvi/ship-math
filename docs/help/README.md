# ShipMath help center

Hello and welcome! These guides will show you how to use ShipMath to take control of the shipping options your customers see at checkout.

You will not need any technical knowledge. Every guide uses plain English, and you will be able to follow along one click at a time.

## Start here

If you are new, open [Getting started](getting-started.md) first. In about ten minutes, it will walk you through opening the app, creating your first zone, building your first rule, and seeing it live at checkout.

## Everyday guides

Once you know the basics, these guides will cover the day to day work:

- [Zones](zones.md) will show you how to save a group of destinations, like "UK only" or "Europe", so you can reuse it in any rule.
- [Shipping rules](rules.md) will show you how to hide, rename, move, or reprice delivery options at checkout.
- [Syncing your changes](sync.md) will explain how your saved rules reach the checkout, and what the warnings mean.
- [Test mode and evaluation mode](test-mode.md) will help you try new ideas safely, and choose how many rules apply to an order.
- [FAQ](faq.md) has short answers about uninstalling, limits, and what happens when things go wrong.

## The app at a glance

You will find a small menu at the top of every page:

| Page | What you will do there |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | See all your rules, check sync health, and spot the test mode state |
| Zones | Manage the groups of destinations your rules can target |
| Settings | Turn test mode on or off and control when carrier rates go live |
| Contact | Send a message when you are stuck |

## Two ideas that explain everything

Almost everything in ShipMath follows two simple ideas:

1. A **zone** answers one question: where should this apply?
2. A **rule** answers the other: what should happen there?

If you remember only these two sentences, the rest of the guides will feel easy.
