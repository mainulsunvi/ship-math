# ShipMath help center

Welcome! These guides show you how to use ShipMath to take control of the shipping options your customers see at checkout.

You do not need any technical knowledge. Every guide uses plain English and walks you through each step, one click at a time.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) covers your first ten minutes: opening the app, creating your first zone, building your first rule, and making it live at checkout.

## Everyday guides

- [Zones](zones.md): save a group of destinations, like "UK only" or "Europe", and reuse it in any rule.
- [Shipping rules](rules.md): hide, rename, move, or reprice delivery options at checkout.
- [Syncing your changes](sync.md): learn how your saved rules reach the checkout, and what the warnings mean.
- [Test mode and evaluation mode](test-mode.md): try new ideas safely, and choose how many rules apply to an order.
- [FAQ](faq.md): short answers about uninstalling, limits, and what happens when things go wrong.

## The app at a glance

ShipMath has a simple menu at the top of every page:

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | See all your rules, check sync health, and switch test mode on or off |
| Zones | Manage the groups of destinations your rules can target |
| Settings | General settings will arrive with the next release |
| Contact | Send a message when you are stuck |

## Two ideas that explain everything

Almost everything in ShipMath follows two simple ideas:

1. A **zone** answers the question: where should this apply?
2. A **rule** answers the question: what should happen there?

If you remember only these two sentences, the rest of the guides will feel easy.
